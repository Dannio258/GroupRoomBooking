Group Room Booking — NTI Gymnasiet Södertörn

Find and book available group rooms quickly and easily.

About

This booking system is made for students at NTI Gymnasiet Södertörn who need a quiet place for group work, meetings or focused study. Check available rooms, choose a time and manage your bookings in one place.

How It Works

Browse available group rooms, select a suitable date and time, and confirm your booking. You can also view your existing reservations and keep track of when and where your group is meeting.
